# LaTeX: inline and display math

Euler's identity $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$ is inline. The quadratic formula:

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

Chat-style delimiters also work: \( \alpha + \beta = \gamma \) and

\[
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
\]

A matrix:

$$
\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix} x \\ y \end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix} ax + by \\ cx + dy \end{pmatrix}
$$

Sums and limits: $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, $\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1$.

Money is not math: the plan costs $5 and the upgrade costs $10 per month.
