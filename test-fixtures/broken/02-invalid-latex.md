# Formulas that cannot render

Undefined command: $\notacommand{x}$

Unbalanced braces: $\frac{1}{2$

Mismatched delimiters: $\left( x \right.$

A display formula with an environment that never closes:

$$
\begin{aligned} a &= b
$$

Valid math still renders: $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$

Text after the formulas must still render.
